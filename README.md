# Bitcoin Market Sentiment vs Trader Performance Analysis

## Submitted By
Jyotika Punshi

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to explore the relationship between Bitcoin market sentiment (Fear & Greed Index) and trader performance using historical trading data from Hyperliquid. By combining market sentiment with trading records, this project aims to uncover hidden patterns, evaluate trader behavior under different market conditions, and provide actionable insights that can support smarter trading strategies.

## Tools & Libraries

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings

- The analysis showed that market sentiment has an impact on trading activity, with most trades taking place during Fear and Greed market conditions.
- Trader profitability changed across different market sentiments, suggesting that market conditions can influence trading performance.
- The buying and selling patterns were also different for each sentiment category, indicating that traders adjust their decisions based on the market.
- The Closed PnL distribution showed that while most trades resulted in moderate profits or losses, a few trades had significantly higher profits or losses.
- The correlation analysis helped identify how factors such as trade size, execution price, and fees are related to trading performance.
- The analysis also showed that not all cryptocurrencies performed the same, as some coins generated better average returns than others.

## Business Recommendations

- Based on the analysis, it can be seen that market sentiment plays an important role in trading performance. Keeping track of market sentiment before placing trades may help traders make better decisions.

- During highly volatile market conditions, following proper risk management practices can help reduce the chances of large losses.

- Traders can also consider adjusting their trading strategy and position size according to the current market conditions instead of using the same approach every time.

- The analysis also shows that some cryptocurrencies performed better than others, so focusing on consistently better-performing coins while monitoring the market could be beneficial.

- Finally, combining market sentiment with technical analysis may provide a better understanding of the market and support more informed trading decisions.

## Conclusion

This project explored the relationship between Bitcoin market sentiment and trader performance by combining historical trading data with the Fear & Greed Index.

From the analysis, it was observed that market sentiment influences trading activity, profitability, and buying and selling behavior. The visualizations showed that trader performance varies under different market conditions and that some cryptocurrencies perform better than others.

Overall, this analysis shows that market sentiment can be a useful factor when making trading decisions. Combining sentiment analysis with proper risk management and technical analysis can help traders make more informed decisions and improve their overall trading strategy.
